# 🛒 Retail Sales Performance Analysis

> An exploratory data analysis (EDA) project on retail sales data to uncover
> spending patterns, top-selling products, and purchase behaviour —
> delivering actionable insights to optimize sales strategy and inventory planning.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Retail businesses generate massive amounts of transactional data but often
struggle to extract meaningful patterns from it. This project answers:

- Which products and categories drive the most revenue?
- What are the peak purchase periods and customer spending patterns?
- Which purchase modes (online/offline) perform better?
- How can inventory planning be improved based on sales trends?

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

- **File:** `retail_store_sales.csv`
- **Cleaned File:** `cleaned_data.csv`
- **Type:** Retail transaction records
- **Key columns:** Product category, sales amount, purchase mode, date, customer info

## 🔍 Methodology

1. **Data Cleaning** — Handled missing values, corrected data types, removed duplicates
2. **Exploratory Analysis** — Univariate and bivariate analysis of key variables
3. **Visualisation** — Bar charts, heatmaps, trend lines to surface patterns
4. **Insight Generation** — Translated findings into business recommendations

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- **Finding 1:** Butchers contributed 13.2% of total revenue, making it the highest-performing product segment.
- **Finding 2:** Online purchases accounted for 50.5% of transactions, outperforming in-store purchases by about 1.1%.
- **Finding 3:** Sales peaked in January, generating ₹180,398 in revenue (~11.2% of total sales), suggesting an opportunity for seasonal promotional campaigns during this period.
